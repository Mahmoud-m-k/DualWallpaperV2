> [!NOTE]
> **This is a fork of [Yanndroid/DualWallpaper](https://github.com/Yanndroid/DualWallpaper) that adds wallpaper positioning and zoom.**

## Why this fork exists

DualWallpaper is a great idea, but the original app hasn't been updated in over two years. One thing that always frustrated me was that there was no way to choose how your wallpaper is positioned or zoomed. The app just scaled every image to fill the screen and cropped it around the center, so if the part of the photo you cared about wasn't in the middle, there was nothing you could do. So I decided to add it myself.

### What's new

- **Position and zoom your wallpapers.** After picking an image, a new screen lets you drag it around and pinch to zoom inside a frame shaped like your screen, so you choose exactly what ends up as the wallpaper. Double tap or press **Reset** to go back to centered.
- **Adjust position later.** Tap any wallpaper preview and choose **Adjust position** to reframe it whenever you want. The app keeps the full original image, so you never lose the parts that were cropped out.
- **Works everywhere you set an image.** Picking a new image, using your current wallpaper, and sharing a photo to the app from your gallery all open the positioning screen.
- **Photos show up the right way up.** Photos taken in portrait on your camera used to come out sideways; they now respect their rotation.

> The Google Play link below is for the original app, which doesn't include these changes. To get this version, build it from source with Android Studio.

Customize your device even more by having two separate sets of wallpapers for light and dark mode. When your device's theme changes, this app will automatically change your wallpaper to the one you've selected. It is also possible to switch wallpapers based on the time.

You can chose an image, a plain color or a gradient for each wallpaper (light/dark and lockscreen/homescreen). Those will then be used as wallpapers when the device's theme changes or the choosen time is reached.

And all of this for free and without ads!
